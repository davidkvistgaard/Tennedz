# Pelotonia visual geography 1.6

The owner's [visual specification](./PELOTONIA_VISUAL_GEOGRAPHY_SPEC_V1_6.md) governs the atlas built from the unchanged [World 1.5](./PELOTONIA-WORLD-1.5.md) object graph. `lib/world/visual-style.mjs` holds palette, label and zoom rules keyed to established IDs; `lib/world/atlas-data.mjs` holds **prototype representations** of those same objects. `lib/world/visual-validate.mjs` checks the finished atlas on import. Nothing in V1.6 is a new geographic source or a cycling world layer.

The whole-island map uses a deep-to-shallow ocean treatment, coastal shelf, continuous land fill, 13 region-specific palettes, relief and woodland marks, glacier/lake/rim/canyon forms, nine city labels and national road/rail. Secondary networks and settlements appear as the camera approaches L2. Aurelia's existing districts and cathedral detail remain the L3/L4 reference. The grid is hidden in normal play and can be toggled as a debug overlay independently of the map camera.

The five previously generic southeastern island drawings have been replaced by presentations of existing named `V12-ISLAND-*` and named smaller-island objects. The Pelotonia Link uses a 40 km **illustrative** land–water–island line along the existing mainland-to-Mara corridor; it remains a concept in World 1.5. The Crown Reservoir and Great Caldera rim likewise use illustrative geometries on their existing IDs. Their shapes, like the coastline, city footprints and routes, are design proposals rather than surveyed geography. The visual checker verifies the link's land endpoints and water crossing, lake containment within the caldera, dam–river–reservoir intersection, valid ice placement, nine major cities, region-style coverage, L1 network disclosure, label priority and absence of default grid seams.

The map currently offers detailed site footprints in Aurelia and coarse, source-linked map forms elsewhere. Exact regional forest boundaries, pass/road curves, local building footprints and seasonal effects remain unauthored because the specifications do not supply coordinates. Places without a defensible map position remain discoverable through the atlas directory and hierarchy rather than acquiring an invented point. Dynamic weather and seasonal overlays can be added independently of the base geography later.

Verification: run `node --test tests/unit/*.test.mjs`, `eslint app lib tests`, `node scripts/build-world-systems.mjs --check`, the atlas browser tests and `next build`. The versioned World 1.0–1.5 datasets and their tests remain intact.
