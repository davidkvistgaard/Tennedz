# Pelotonia World Baseline 1.6.1 — source reconciliation

This is an errata and audit layer over the existing World 1.5 object graph and Visual Geography 1.6 atlas. It does not renumber stable repository IDs, create a parallel geographic dataset, or add cycling content. The implemented repository state is the technical baseline; the original source documents remain historical evidence where they conflict with the owner's later correction.

## Resolved corrections

- V1.3 retains the meanings of its `DISC-*` source numbers. The 18 colliding headings in the checked-in V1.4 source now use `DISC-120`–`DISC-137`, following the owner's exact name-to-number mapping. Those are **source aliases** to already-reconciled objects, not replacements for object IDs. The established `V14-SITE-DISC-008-*` and similar child IDs remain stable. The parser rejects a future reuse of a V1.3 source number for a different V1.4 place.
- `GRID-K3` is Northern Plateau (`REG-NORTHERN-PLATEAU`); `GRID-O3` is ocean. V1.1 takes precedence over the conflicting V1.2 labels.
- `RC-010` Aurel Gate (~41,000), `RC-018` Redgate (~61,000) and `RC-019` Saltwell (~37,000) use wider settlement/municipal populations. Their earlier ~1,100 / ~21,000 / ~8,000 estimates are recorded as **core-settlement subsets**, not additional population.
- Three clear duplicate representations retain both IDs with an explicit `canonicalFeatureId` and `alias_of` link: `V15-JUNC-CROWN` → `AUR-14-V12-PLACE-06` (Crown Junction), `V12-NAT-GREAT-RANGE-AURELIA-MASSIF-24` → `V12-005-002` (Serac Valley), and `INF-003-V14-04` → `HYD-RES-CROWN` (Crown Reservoir). The route keeps its existing junction waypoint and geometry.

## Automated audit

Run `node scripts/audit-world-baseline.mjs`. It validates the 1.0, 1.3, 1.4 and 1.5 datasets and the 1.6 atlas, including the 192 grid cells, parent/relation and zoom references, regional membership, topological road/rail/ferry and hydrology rules, source alias uniqueness, preservation of every earlier stable ID, corrected `DISC-*` identity, population scopes and visual geography. Unit tests call the same audit. Exact network lines and place geometry remain explicitly labelled prototypes.

The complete audit also reviewed names that recur under the same parent, and duplicate-name clusters elsewhere. A shared label alone does not prove two different physical features are identical: a road and a settlement corridor may appropriately share a name. The cases below require world-design decisions before identity or parent changes.

Generic names such as “Old Centre”, “Station” and “Harbour” occur under different towns and are treated as separate local places. `Greywall` and `Ternvik` already carry canonical identity links. The Western/Southern and Eastern settlement corridors share labels with roads but represent different types of object. Two `Kaen–Redgate` road legs belong to different national route definitions and remain separate route segments.

## Decisions still needed

1. **Great Caldera and its lake.** `DISC-012` is named “Great Caldera” but currently typed as a lake under `REG-007`; `PHY-011` is “Great Caldera Lake” under `WORLD-001`. Eight rim, shore and island names occur as separate children of both objects. V1.6 visually uses a caldera rim around the lake. Choose whether Great Caldera should be the parent caldera/landform containing Great Caldera Lake, with those repeated children reconciled by identity, or whether the two are intentionally distinct lake-related catalogues. No IDs need be deleted in either choice.
2. **Deepfjord and Sealholm.** In `REG-011`, Deepfjord has both a `site_area` and a `fjord` record; Sealholm has both a `site_area` and a `settlement` record. Choose whether each `site_area` is an area around the physical fjord/settlement, or just an older representation of that same feature. The choice determines parent and alias relationships.
3. **Kaen–Red River outlet.** V1.5's prose allows either a terminal lower-desert river or a southeastern-coast outlet depending on seasonal discharge; the current prototype assumes an `ocean-mouth` for `HYD-R04`. Choose whether this river reaches the sea every year, only in wet years, or ordinarily terminates inland. The answer determines the river's terminal semantics and seasonal rendering.
4. **National population.** The twelve original regional estimates sum to ~12.1 million, which already matches the country's ~12.1 million headline approximately, but Northern Plateau has no assigned regional estimate. Choose whether the plateau's residents are already included in neighbouring regional estimates or whether to revise the regional distribution while retaining the national total. City and core-settlement figures remain subsets and must not be summed into the total separately.
5. **Other overlapping named sites.** “Cathedral Gardens” exists both under Old Aurelia (`AUR-SITE-003`) and under the cathedral (`AUR-V12-CATH-06`). “Crown Pass” and “Black Needle” each appear both as a regional site and as a child of a named mountain. “Rainwall Viaduct” exists as a Westhaven landmark and as an Emerald Coast fixed connection; “West Reef” appears under two nearby island/lagoon parents. Decide whether each pair is the same place shown at different scopes (then retain IDs and add alias/containment links), or distinct places that happen to share a name. No parent or identity is changed until that is specified.

The six provisional V1.5 settlement-cell revisions remain as implemented, with their previous cells recorded in `placementRevision`. They are schematic network waypoints, not exact surveyed locations. Final placements and the Pelotonia Link's precise alignment can be decided during later cartographic work; they are not silently changed by this baseline.
