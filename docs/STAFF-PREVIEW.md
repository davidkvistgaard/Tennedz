# Staff and membership design preview

The agreed business principle is free access to every sporting staff role and equivalent sporting possibilities. Supporter may offer cosmetic staff customisation, longer history, extra saved templates, bulk editing and club storytelling. It must not increase training effectiveness, recovery speed or sporting staff capacity. Exact entitlements remain planned, not purchasable.

/team/staff is a protected, English-only local design preview. Six role concepts: head coach, specialist coach, conditioning coach, physiotherapist, sports psychologist and youth coach. These are not assigned employees; no fabricated contracts, wages or skill ratings are presented as real data.

Users can try distinct men’s and women’s training directions. State exists only while the page is mounted and resets on reload/navigation. The page explicitly describes this limitation. No storage, API writes, subscriptions or economy/engine effects. Shared staff capacity and real individual plans are deferred until training and economy design resumes.

Future staff records should distinguish expertise, teaching, youth development, teamwork, specialty, salary and contract. Form, long-term skill development, fatigue/recovery and mental attributes require separately defined mechanics. The new engine remains paused.

Validation: ESLint, production build and all 24 browser journeys passed. After a small squad-button CSS correction, build and both staff journeys passed again. Mobile/desktop screenshots reviewed. No production changes.
