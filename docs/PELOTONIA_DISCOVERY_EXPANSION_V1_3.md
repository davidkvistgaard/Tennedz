# PELOTONIA DISCOVERY EXPANSION V1.3

> Extend World 1.0 + V1.1 + V1.2. Reconcile rather than duplicate. No cycling-specific content.

## Purpose
Make random exploration rewarding with secondary cities, regional discoveries and L3/L4 anchors while preserving genuine wilderness.

## Rules
- Names are working `canonical_editable` names unless an existing object already has another status.
- Every settlement must have a geographic reason: harbour, river crossing, lake shore, pass, water source, transport junction or buildable plain.
- L2 shows regionally important objects; L3 local discoveries; L4 only genuinely distinctive sites.
- Empty space is intentional. Do not clone the same POI pattern across grids.
- Preserve stable IDs and continuous geometry from earlier world versions.

# 4. Discovery clusters
## Red Canyon cluster
Connect Redgate, Saltwell, Mesa Crossing and Dusthaven through canyon/plateau roads. Resolve Cathedral Gorge, Copper Narrows, Scarlet Walls, Seven Mesas, Sun Arch, Salt Mirror, Dry Cathedral and Black Wash. Canyon Bridge is an L4 engineering candidate.

## Emerald waterfall cluster
Crownfall, Seven Veils, Cloudfall, Moss Gorge, Rainwall Falls, Fern Cascade and Green Stair form one coherent orographic waterfall country. Greenfall is the principal urban gateway.

## Great Caldera cluster
Great Caldera Lake, North Rim, Black Rim, Ember Rim, Collapsed East Gate, Steam Shore, Obsidian Peninsula, Ash Islands, White Terraces, Black Geyser Field and Glass Plain. Ember is the principal gateway.

## Southeastern archipelago
Mara Island/Maravista, Tern Island/Ternport, Beacon Island/Beacon City, Pelican Key, Coral Cay, Long Isle, Twin Islands, Reefholm and South Key. Maintain channels, ferry ring and Pelotonia Link corridor as continuous spatial objects.

## Southern Fjords
Greyfjord, Deepfjord, Southwatch Sound, Tern Fjord, Icewater Reach and Seal Sound form the major fjord system. Greyhaven is the principal service city.

# 5. Deep-zoom templates
**Regional city:** 3–6 districts plus 3–6 anchors; centre/old core, transport or waterfront node, park/public landscape, civic/cultural landmark and one feature explaining why the city exists.

**Town:** centre, station/harbour if relevant, square/park, one distinctive local landmark and surrounding river/coast/valley context.

**Natural landmark:** geometry + access/viewpoint + adjacent terrain at L3; subfeatures only at L4 for major sites.

**Engineering landmark:** approaches/junctions/service areas at L3; individual spans, portals, locks, stations or visitor structures at L4.

**Wilderness:** peaks, valleys, glaciers, rivers, forest blocks and protected areas; do not fabricate settlements.

# 6. Definition of done
1. Merge into versioned world data without duplicating V1.0–V1.2 objects.
2. Assign stable IDs and parents to all new cities/discoveries.
3. Place objects only where geography supports them.
4. Add district/landmark children to the 24 regional cities.
5. Preserve continuous transport, hydrology and island geometry.
6. Make progressive visibility data-driven at L2/L3/L4.
7. Run world validation, tests, lint and production build.
8. Report all collisions/reconciliations and unresolved placements.
9. Do not invent history/lore or cycling content.
