# Pelotonia World Baseline 1.6.2 — owner decisions

This is an editorial correction layer over [World Baseline 1.6.1](./PELOTONIA-WORLD-BASELINE-1.6.1.md), the World 1.5 object graph and the Visual Geography 1.6 renderer. The owner selected all five proposed resolutions on 30 September 2026. Earlier source specifications remain historical evidence; their established repository IDs are not renamed or deleted. The `datasetVersion` stays `1.5` because this layer reconciles meaning rather than introducing a parallel schema or geography.

1. **Great Caldera** (`DISC-012`) is the caldera landform in the Volcanic Basin. **Great Caldera Lake** (`PHY-011`) is its contained lake. Blue Depths and Ash Islands belong to the lake; rims, shores and other volcanic landforms belong to the caldera. The eight V1.4 children that repeat V1.2 names retain their IDs as `alias_of` references to the earlier physical features.
2. **Deepfjord** (`V12-011-003` and `V12-NAT-SOUTHERN-FJORDS-02`) identifies one fjord. **Sealholm** (`V12-011-005` and `V12-NAT-SOUTHERN-FJORDS-13`) identifies one settlement. The older generic `site_area` entries remain addressable aliases, not separate named surrounding areas.
3. **Kaen–Red River** (`RIV-004`, source alias `HYD-R04`) has a continuous channel but reaches the southeastern sea only in wet years. Its lower reach (`HYD-R04-SEG-03`) is seasonal. The atlas renders that reach with a dashed line. The local-km line remains a prototype; this decision does not survey a precise river mouth.
4. **Northern Plateau** has approximately 300,000 residents. Northern Highlands changes from approximately 1,250,000 to 1,100,000 and Central Plains from approximately 3,650,000 to 3,500,000. The 13 regional estimates therefore still total approximately 12,100,000. Settlement counts, including Stoneheath and Windmere, are regional subsets and are never added again to the national total. World 1.0 retains its original unknown Plateau estimate as versioned source history.
5. **Cathedral Gardens, Crown Pass, Black Needle, Rainwall Viaduct and West Reef** each identify one physical place across their two source contexts. Their existing IDs are preserved with `canonicalFeatureId` and `alias_of` links. The atlas directory presents the canonical feature once. Cathedral Gardens is placed under the cathedral precinct in the reconciled hierarchy.

`lib/world/baseline-decisions.mjs` applies these decisions to cloned World 1.5 objects. `scripts/audit-world-baseline.mjs` checks every identity link, caldera hierarchy, seasonal outlet, population total and visual seasonal reach alongside the existing ID, source, grid, zoom and network audit. Run that audit, `npm test`, `npm run lint` and `npm run build` after changes. Exact cartographic positions and the other provisional network geometry remain future art-direction work.
